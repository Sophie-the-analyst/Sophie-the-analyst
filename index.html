What the data suggests

Three takeaways worth noticing.

A good first analysis balances interesting patterns with honest limits on what the data can tell us.

INSIGHT 01

DLC Garden leads this sample

Eight respondents (22.2%) named DLC Garden, making it the most frequently named favourite in this survey—not necessarily the most popular spot across the whole university.

INSIGHT 02

Relaxation matters

Relaxing or hanging out was the most common use category (17 mentions, 47.2%). Quietness, scenery, comfort, food, and social connection also appeared in respondents’ reasons.

INSIGHT 03

Favourite spots are part of routine

20 respondents (55.6%) said they visit almost every day or several times a week, suggesting these places may be woven into students’ regular campus life.

A note on limitations
This is a small, voluntary sample of 36 responses, with 500-level students and Pharmaceutical Sciences students overrepresented. The findings are descriptive and should not be treated as representative of all ABU students. Spot-type responses can overlap, and spending responses do not support a reliable average.
